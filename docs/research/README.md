# Research

Research contains authored evidence syntheses and the immutable source material they draw on.

- `raw/` is the workspace source corpus. New material enters at its top level.
- `raw/ingested/` holds sources whose ideas have been incorporated into concepts or other durable documents.
- `evidence/` subdirectories hold verbatim captured artifacts supporting an authored report. They are not authored canon and are exempt from repo-relative path checking for the same reason `raw/` sources are: their paths were true in their source context.
- Reports and syntheses live directly in this directory and use topic-specific filenames.

Raw source files are evidence, not current behavior or normative requirements. Keep corrections and interpretation in authored reports, concepts, or plans without rewriting the source.
