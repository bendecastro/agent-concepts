# Research

Research contains authored evidence syntheses and the immutable source material they draw on.

- Each investigation is one folder named `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic>/`, dated by its first commit, with a `README.md` (or a brief and memo) as its entry point.
- `raw/` is the workspace source corpus. New material enters at its top level.
- `raw/ingested/` holds sources whose ideas have been incorporated into concepts or other durable documents.
- `evidence/` subfolders inside an investigation hold verbatim captured artifacts supporting its report. They are not authored canon and are exempt from repo-relative path checking for the same reason `raw/` sources are: their paths were true in their source context.
