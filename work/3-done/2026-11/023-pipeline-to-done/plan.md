## Outcome

`bc-drain-issues` is now `bc-pipeline-to-done` (old name kept as a deploy alias) and builds both work items marked `Ready for agent: yes` and `ready-for-agent` GitHub issues from one shared core. Work items are claimed whole (claim branch with a remote; with no remote, one driver and the committed move to active), built step by step with review, and parked with a `Waiting on owner:` question when a decision is needed. `bc-plan-to-pipeline` writes the ready line and `work_index.py` shows ready and waiting items. The dry-run pressure test held after three wording fixes, with lift in every scenario ([results](../../../../concepts/bc-pipeline-to-done/tests/pressure-work-items-results-2026-11-25.md)). The README presents building with your agent present as the standard way and the skill as the unattended option with its review cost. The first real work-item drain is the live test.
