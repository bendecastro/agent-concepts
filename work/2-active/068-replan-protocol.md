# A work item shows when it is being replanned, and a review loop stops in every mode

Why: when a plan turns out wrong (two or more blocking reviews, a failed pressure test, the owner changing scope), the item keeps looking normal: it sits in `2-active` with ticked steps and stale notes while rounds of rework run, and the real story lives only in run folders. The "stop after two blocking reviews on new sequences" rule exists only in the private swarm-mode prompt, so `bc-code-review`, `bc-subagent-driven-development` and `bc-pipeline-to-done` (which has its own three-cycle cap) never apply it, and nothing says how to return an item to planning: sessions improvised "Replan note:" and "Not planned yet" lines. Evidence: the study in the run folder `2026-11-28-replan-experience` (this repo's 067, 058, 055 and 060 loops, and another project's items that went through six blocking reviews, two replans and an owner-ordered split).
Done when: On the pressure-test model, a session that has just received a second blocking review naming a new sequence stops dispatching fixes, writes `Replanning:`, a Replan note and the cumulative `Rounds:` line in the item, keeps landed steps ticked with any superseded step marked, and asks the owner recommending replanning, 3 of 3, with master failing at least once; work_index shows the new state and rejects `Replanning:` with `Ready for agent:`.

## Requirements

Owner decisions (2026-11-28): the item stays in its folder while replanned; the dashboard shows the new lines (its own item in the dashboard project).

- **Replanning line.** `Replanning: <one-line reason> — decides: owner|agent` in the item's header lines (in `plan.md` for a folder item). The item stays in its current folder. It never coexists with `Ready for agent:`; writing it removes that line. When the owner decides, the question goes where the owner sees questions (as for `Waiting on owner:`); `Waiting on owner:` may stand next to `Replanning:` for the question itself. The drain's existing park-to-planned rule stays for plain owner decisions that are not replans.
- **Replan notes.** A `## Replan notes` section, one entry per replan, newest last: date; trigger (which reviews or tests, and the unmodelled states, events, orderings or version pairings they exposed, each with the outcome it must reach); the owner's choice; what changed in Requirements and Steps.
- **Steps during a replan.** Landed steps stay ticked; never untick. A step whose result the replan invalidates gets an indented `Superseded <date>: <why>; replaced by <step title>` note. Scope cut from the item becomes a new planned item without a `Planned:` line ("not planned yet") or an ideas line; a dropped slice that was its own item goes to dropped with its reason. Stale pending notes under a step are rewritten, not left.
- **Rounds line.** `Rounds: <n> blocking reviews, <m> replans` in the header, cumulative across replans (counts never restart), added at the first blocking review and kept through done.
- **One stop rule, one home.** Move the review-loop stop rule into canon in one place (`bc-code-review`'s handling of review feedback is the expected home; settle it with lint's repeated-text check) and merge `bc-pipeline-to-done`'s three-cycle cap into it: a failed fix twice still defers as today; two blocking reviews in a row on new sequences stop fixer dispatch even under ship-without-asking instructions, write the Replanning line and note, optionally ask an oracle, and ask the owner recommending a return to planning. `bc-subagent-driven-development` and `bc-pipeline-to-done` point at it (`loads` edges in `relationships.json`).
- **Resume.** `/bc-plan-to-pipeline` on an item with `Replanning:` plans it in place wherever it sits: it reads the Replan notes, rewrites Requirements and adds or supersedes steps, removes `Replanning:` and restores `Ready for agent: yes` when the readiness bar is met again.
- **Index and data.** `work_index.py` shows a replanning item in the Folder column like a waiting one, errors on `Replanning:` plus `Ready for agent:`, and `--json` exposes `replanning` (reason, decider) and `rounds`; `work-data.md` documents them. The scaffold's `.bc-agent/AGENTS.md` work-item rules describe the lines; a MIGRATIONS entry tells existing projects.
- **Test.** Pressure scenario: an active item after one blocking review that was fixed, then a second blocking review naming a new sequence, with a scripted "just fix it and ship" message. Grade the item file and reply from artifacts. Master vs changed, 3 runs each, Terra low (`openai-codex/gpt-6.1-terra`) via `scripts/pi_sandbox.py`; quota exhaustion is PARTIAL, do not substitute without recording it.
